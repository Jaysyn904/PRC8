Epic Brimstone Speakers are divine orators of sacred flame, their voices echoing with the resonance of celestial truth. Their mastery of truenames reaches apocalyptic potency—capable of invoking divine conflagrations that sear away corruption and summon angelic paragons from the farthest reaches of the Upper Planes. When an Epic Brimstone Speaker speaks, the heavens answer, fire obeys, and evil trembles before the purity of their word-forged wrath.

Hit Die: d8.
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Intelligence modifier.
Bonus Feats: The epic Brimstone Speaker gains a bonus feat every three levels.

Special:
Truenaming: The epic Brimstone Speaker continues to increase in Truenaming level.
Spellcasting: At each odd class level the epic Brimstone Speaker continues to increase in divine caster level (and spells known, if applicable).
