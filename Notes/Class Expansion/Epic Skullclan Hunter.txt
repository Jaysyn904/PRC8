An epic Skullclan Hunter is the eternal bane of the undead. With eyes that pierce through shadow and a resolve that rivals divine judgment, they tread where others dare not — into tombs reeking with the chill of unlife. Each strike of their blade sears with holy vengeance, bypassing foul defenses and undoing necromantic abominations with grace and fury. Immune to the withering touch of unlife, they become a living bulwark against the darkness; a radiant scourge born to end what should never have risen.

Hit Die: d6
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 6 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Skullclan Hunter gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Sneak Attack: The epic Skullclan Hunter's sneak attack continues to increase by 1d6 every 3 levels.
