At epic heights, the Blood Magus is no mere spellcaster—they are blood given will, a living paradox of life dancing on the edge of death. Their veins pulse with raw arcane potential, and their mastery over their own mortality is unmatched. To them, blood is not just life—it is power, memory, and magic itself. 

Hit Die: d6
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Blood Magus gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Durable Casting: the epic Blood Magus continues to gain +1 bouns per class level to Concentration checks.
Homunculus: the epic Blood Magus' Homunculus continues to scale in power with the Blood Magus' class level.
Spellcasting: At every even level, the epic Blood Magus continues to increase in arcane caster level (and spells known, if applicable).

Epic Blood Magus Bonus Feat List:
Automatic Quicken Spell, Automatic Silent Spell, Automatic Still Spell, Blinding Speed, Craft Epic Magic Arms and Armor, Craft Epic Staff, Craft Epic Wondrous Item, Epic Leadership, Epic Spell Focus, Epic Spell Penetration, Epic Spellcasting, Epic Toughness, Fast Healing, Focused Spell Penetration, Forge Epic Ring, Great Charisma, Great Constitution, Great Intelligence, Ignore Material Components, Improved Combat Casting, Tenacious Magic.
