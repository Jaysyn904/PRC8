At epic levels, your mastery in dismantling arcane defenses and countering spellcasters reaches its zenith. You have honed both your martial prowess and your specialized anti-magic spells to a razor’s edge, exposing and exploiting the vulnerabilities of even the most formidable mages.

Hit Die: d4
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Intelligence modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Mage Killer gains a bonus feat every two levels

Improved Saves: The epic mage killer's training continues improving one of their weak saves each level.
Spells: The epic mage killer increases caster level by one per level gained above 10th.
