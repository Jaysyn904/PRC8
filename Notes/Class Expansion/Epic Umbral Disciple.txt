Mastering the darkness within and without, the Epic Umbral Disciple becomes a living shadow, slipping unseen through the world and striking with spectral precision. They transcend physical limitations, extending their reach beyond mortal constraints and channeling pure shadow essence into devastating attacks.

Hit Die: d6
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 6 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Umbral Disciple gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Sneak Attack: The epic unseen seer's sneak attack continues to increase by 1d6 every 3 levels.
Meldshaping: Increases as if gaining a level in your meldshaper class at each level.
