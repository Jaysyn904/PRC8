As an Epic Alienist, the boundaries of reality and sanity become mere illusions. With a mind forever altered by the truths of the Far Realm, they no longer walk among mortals but drift between dimensions, communing with incomprehensible entities.

Hit Die: d4
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Alienist gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Caster Level: Increases as if gaining a level in your arcane spellcasting class.

Epic Alienist Bonus Feat List:
Automatic Quicken Spell, Automatic Silent Spell, Automatic Still Spell, Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Craft Epic Magic Arms and Armor, Craft Epic Rod, Craft Epic Staff, Craft Epic Wondrous Item, Epic Damage Resistance, Epic Spell Focus, Epic Spell Penetration, Epic Spellcasting, Epic Toughness, Fast Healing, Focused Spell Penetration, Forge Epic Ring, Great Charisma, Great Intelligence, Ignore Material Components, Improved Combat Casting, Tenacious Magic.
