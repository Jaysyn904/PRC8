Epic Hands of the Winged Masters become more than servants—they become whispers in the wind, shadows beneath wings, and the unseen daggers of draconic will. Their bond to their draconic patrons transcends mere loyalty, transforming into something sacred and absolute. As spies, saboteurs, and assassins, they move unseen through the world, wielding deadly grace and dragon-blessed cunning. Each word they speak and every blade they draw serves the ancient agendas of their winged masters with terrifying precision.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 6 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Hands of the Winged Masters gains a bonus feat every three levels.

Special:
Special Attack: the epic Hand of the Winged Masters's sneak attack or skirmish attack continues to gain +1d6 damage every three levels.

Epic Hands of the Winged Masters Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Epic Dodge, Epic Prowess, Epic Reputation, Epic Skill Focus, Epic Trapfinding, Epic Weapon Focus, Great Charisma, Great Dexterity, Great Intelligence, Improved Sneak Attack, Lingering Damage, Psionic Feats, Self-Concealment, Superior Initiative.
