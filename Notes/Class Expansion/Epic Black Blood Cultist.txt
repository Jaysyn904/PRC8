As an Epic Black Blood Cultist, the savage spirit of the Beastlord fully consumes you, transforming you into a walking nightmare of claws, fangs, and unrelenting fury. Your connection to the Black Blood deepens, making your body tougher, your instincts sharper, and your ferocity unmatched. 

Hit Die: d12
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Black Blood Cultist gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Feral rage: The epic Black Blood Cultist continues to gain 1 use of rage every 4 class levels.

Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Damage Reduction, Devastating Critical, Epic Prowess, Epic Toughness, Epic Weapon Focus, Fast Healing, Legendary Wrestler, Mighty Rage, Overwhelming Critical, Superior Initiative, Terrifying Rage, Thundering Rage.
