The epic Disciple of Baalzebul is a master of lies, deception, and treachery. She weaves webs of manipulation, using guile and cunning to achieve her goals. A true servant of the Lord of Lies, she excels in deception, charming others to her will, summoning infernal allies, and calling upon swarms of vile insects to torment her enemies.

Hit Die: d6
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 6 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Disciple of Baalzebu gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Sneak Attack: The epic Disciple of Baalzebu's sneak attack continues to increase by 1d6 every 3 levels.

Epic Disciple of Baalzebu Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Energy Resistance, Fast Healing, Perfect Health, Epic Dodge, Epic Prowess, Epic Reputation, Epic Skill Focus, Great Charisma, Improved Sneak Attack, Lingering Damage, Self-Concealment, Superior Initiative.
