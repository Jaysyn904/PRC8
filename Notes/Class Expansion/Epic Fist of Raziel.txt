Epic Fists of Raziel become unstoppable champions of celestial wrath, striking down evil with overwhelming divine power. Their Smite Evil grows stronger, their holy strikes more potent, and their weapons become pure instruments of justice.

Hit Die: d4
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Fist of Raziel gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Spellcasting: The epic Fist of Raziel continues to increase in caster level (and spells known, if applicable).
