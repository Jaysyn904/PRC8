:: Epic Hathran ::

At epic levels, your mastery of both arcane and divine magic crowns you as an exalted Hathran ?a beacon of healing and leadership in Rashemen. You embody the sacred legacy of the learned sisters, extending your nurturing miracles and commanding spells that both protect your people and instill awe in your foes.

Hit Die: d4
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Hathran gains a bonus feat every second level after 10th.

Special:
Spells: The epic Hathran increases divine and arcane caster level by one every other level gained above 10th.
Fear: The epic hathran may use this ability one additional time per day for every 3 levels gained above 9th

Epic Hathran Bonus Feat List:
Automatic Quicken Spell, Automatic Silent Spell, Automatic Still Spell, Craft Epic Magic Arms and Armor, Craft Epic Rod, Craft Epic Staff, Craft Epic Wondrous Item, Epic Leadership, Epic Spell Focus, Epic Spell Penetration, Epic Spellcasting, Forge Epic Ring, Ignore Material Components, Improved Combat Casting, Tenacious Magic.
