:: Epic Master of Shadow ::

Epic Masters of Shadow are no longer mere commanders of darkness-they are its avatars. Their shadow servants swell into entities of primordial gloom, answering unspoken commands with lethal grace. Cloaked in absolute cold and bolstered by unfathomable shadow-magic, these lords of twilight weave darkness into weapons, armor, and essence. In the deep, where no light dares tread, they reign supreme.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Master of Shadow gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Mysteries: The epic Master of Shadow continues to increase in mystery caster level (and mysteries known, if applicable).

Epic Master of Shadow Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Epic Energy Resistance, Epic Self-Concealment, Epic Skill Focus, Favored Mystery, Great Charisma, Greater Path Focus, Nocturnal Caster, Path Focus, Shadow Cast, Superior Initiative, and any metashadow feat.
