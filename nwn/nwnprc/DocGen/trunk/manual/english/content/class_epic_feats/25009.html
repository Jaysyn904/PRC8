:: Epic Unseen Seer ::

At epic levels, you have become the ultimate master of secrets and shadows. Your uncanny ability to blend devastating sneak attacks with potent divinatory insight grows ever sharper.

Hit Die: d4.
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 6 + Intelligence modifier.
Bonus Feats: The epic Unseen Seer gains a bonus feat every three levels.

Special:
Spellcasting: The epic unseen seer continues to increase in caster level (and spells known, if applicable).
Sneak Attack: The epic unseen seer's sneak attack continues to increase by 1d6 every 3 levels.
