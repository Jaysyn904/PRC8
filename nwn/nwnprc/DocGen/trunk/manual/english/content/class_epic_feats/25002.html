:: Epic Binder ::

Epic binders make pacts with the residual presences of beings and cosmic energies so powerful that these energies would drive other beings mad. These binders travel the world and the planes searching for entities forgotten or banished from reality.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: An epic binder gains a bonus feat every three levels higher than 20th (23rd, 26th, 29th, 32nd, 35th, and 38th).
Special:
Vestiges (Soul Binding): Epic binders continue to gain the ability to bind additional vestiges simultaneously. At 25th level, an epic binder can bind 5 vestiges at the same time, increasing by 1 every five binder levels thereafter (6 at 30th, 7 at 35th, and 8 at 40th). Levels taken in epic binder continue to improve binding checks, ability DCs, durations, and other abilities based on effective binder level. An epic binder can bind only one epic vestige at a time unless she takes the Bind Additional Epic Vestige feat.
Pact Augmentation: At 24th level, an epic binder gains a 6th ability from the pact augmentation list, plus an additional ability every four levels thereafter (7 at 28th, 8 at 32nd, 9 at 36th, and 10 at 40th).

Epic Binder Bonus Feat List:
