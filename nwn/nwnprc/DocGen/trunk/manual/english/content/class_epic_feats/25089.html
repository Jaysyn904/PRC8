:: Epic Morninglord of Lathander ::

At epic levels, the Morninglord becomes a living vessel of Lathander's divine light. Their presence alone is a balm to the wounded and a bane to the undead. Their power to turn, heal, and sanctify grows radiant and awe-inspiring, suffused with the hope and renewal of dawn itself. Wherever they walk, darkness falters, and the sun seems to shine a little brighter.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Morninglord of Lathander gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Greater Turning: The epic Morninglord of Lathander continues to gain a use of Greater Turning every four levels.
Spellcasting: The epic Morninglord of Lathander continues to increase in divine caster level (and spells known, if applicable).

Epic Morninglord of Lathander Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Automatic Quicken Spell, Automatic Silent Spell, Automatic Still Spell, Blinding Speed, Epic Divine Might, Epic Divine Resistance, Epic Divine Shield, Epic Divine Vigor, Epic Energy Resistance, Epic Prowess, Epic Spellcasting, Epic Spell Focus, Epic Spell Penetration, Epic Toughness, Epic Weapon Focus, Fast Healing, Focused Spell Penetration, Great Charisma, Great Wisdom, Improved Combat Casting, Invoke Divine Wrath, Perfect Health, Planar Turning, Tenacious Magic.
