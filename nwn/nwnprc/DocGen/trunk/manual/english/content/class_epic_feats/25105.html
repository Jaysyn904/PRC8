:: Epic Master Alchemist ::

An epic Master Alchemist is a living crucible of arcane transmutation, distilling raw magic into liquid form with an artistry that defies mortal comprehension. With hands steady as time and minds sharp as diamond, these arcane chemists brew wonders that rival the greatest of spells , bottled miracles, captured storms, and essence of the gods. Able to craft potions imbued with ninth-circle sorcery, they shape the future in vials and flasks, offering power, salvation, or ruin with a single sip.

Hit Die: d4
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Master Alchemist gains a bonus feat every three levels.

Special:
Spellcasting: At each class level the epic Master Alchemist continues to increase in arcane caster level (and spells known, if applicable).

Epic Master Alchemist Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Automatic Quicken Spell, Automatic Silent Spell, Automatic Still Spell, Blinding Speed, Craft Epic Magic Arms and Armor, Craft Epic Staff, Craft Epic Wondrous Item, Epic Energy Resistance, Epic Leadership, Epic Spell Focus, Epic Spell Penetration, Epic Spellcasting, Epic Skill Focus, Focused Spell Penetration, Forge Epic Ring, Great Charisma, Great Intelligence, Ignore Material Components, Improved Combat Casting, Tenacious Magic. In addition to the feats on this list, the epic Master Alchemist may select any item creation feat not listed here.
