:: Epic Drunken Master ::

The Drunken Master masters the art of unpredictable, staggered movements to confuse and evade enemies. At epic levels, their drunken combat style becomes even more powerful, turning alcohol into a source of strength and resilience. They become a whirlwind of chaos, using their unsteady form to surprise and overpower opponents with unexpected, powerful strikes.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Drunken Master gains a bonus feat every three levels.

Epic Drunken Master Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Damage Reduction, Epic Energy Resistance, Epic Dodge, Epic Prowess, Epic Toughness, Fast Healing, Great Constitution, Great Dexterity, Great Strength, Improved Ki Strike, Improved Spell Resistance, Improved Stunning Fist, Legendary Wrestler, Self-Concealment, Superior Initiative.
