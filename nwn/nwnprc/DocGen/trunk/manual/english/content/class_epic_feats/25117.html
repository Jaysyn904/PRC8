:: Epic Swordsage ::

An epic swordsage is the living embodiment of the Sublime Way, blending shadow, elemental power, and martial grace into a fluid and deadly whole. With every strike, leap, or step, the epic swordsage dances through the battlefield with supernatural precision and fluidity. Their mastery of disciplines allows them to transcend the limits of the body, the blade, and even reality itself.

Hit Die: d8.
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 6 + Intelligence modifier.
Bonus Feats: The epic swordsage gains a bonus feat every three levels.

Special:
Maneuvers: At each class level the epic swordsage continues to increase in initiator level.

Epic swordsage Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Devastating Critical, Epic Damage Reduction, Epic Dodge, Epic Energy Resistance, Epic Prowess, Epic Skill Focus, Epic Toughness, Epic Weapon Focus, Fast Healing, Great Dexterity, Great Wisdom, Improved Stunning Fist, Overwhelming Critical, Perfect Health, Perfect Two-Weapon Fighting, Self Concealment, Superior Initiative, Two-Weapon Rend.
