:: Epic Samurai ::

Having become a master of the Daisho and achieved enlightenment, tales of your bravery and honour have spread far and wide. None dare oppose you or question your will.

Hit Die: d10.
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2+ Int Modifier.
Bonus Feats: The epic samurai gains a bonus feat every three levels after 20th.
