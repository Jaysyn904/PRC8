:: Epic Soulcaster ::

The Epic Soulcaster is a master of both incarnum and arcane (or psionic) energy, seamlessly weaving soul energy into their spells and manifestations. As their mastery deepens, they unlock unparalleled control over chakra binds, enhance their investments to extraordinary levels, and transcend the boundaries between mortal essence and pure magic.

Hit Die: d4
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Soulcaster gains a bonus feat every five levels

Special:
Caster/Manifester/Meldshaper Level: Increases as if gaining a level in both your arcane spellcasting class (or psionic manifester class) and your meldshaper class at each level.

Epic Soulcaster Bonus Feat List:
Automatic Quicken Spell, Automatic Silent Spell, Automatic Still Spell, Bonus Soulmeld, Craft Epic Magic Arms and Armor, Craft Epic Rod, Craft Epic Staff, Craft Epic Wondrous Item, Epic Essentia, Epic Spell Focus, Epic Spell Penetration, Epic Spellcasting, Extra Chakra Bind, Focused Spell Penetration, Forge Epic Ring, Great Charisma, Great Intelligence, Great Wisdom, Ignore Material Components, Improved Combat Casting, Open Heart Chakra, Open Soul Chakra, Rapid Meldshaping, Rebind Soulmeld, Tenacious Magic Soulmanifesters can pick from psionic and metapsionic feats instead of arcane feats.
