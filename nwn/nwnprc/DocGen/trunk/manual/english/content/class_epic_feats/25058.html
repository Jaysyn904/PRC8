:: Epic Red Wizard ::

At epic levels, Red Wizards deepen their mastery over a single school of magic to terrifying heights.

Hit Die: d4
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Red Wizard gains a bonus feat every five levels

Special:
Spell Power: At every even level the epic Red Wizard continues to gain a +1 DC and +1 Spell Penetration on all spells from his spell school of choice.
Specialist Defense: At every even level the epic Red Wizard continues to gain a +1 on saves vs spells in his school of choice.
Spellcasting: The epic Red Wizard continues to increase in caster level (and spells known, if applicable).

Epic Red Wizard Bonus Feat List:
Automatic Quicken Spell, Automatic Silent Spell, Automatic Still Spell, Blinding Speed, Craft Epic Magic Arms and Armor, Craft Epic Staff, Craft Epic Wondrous Item, Epic Leadership, Epic Spell Focus, Epic Spell Penetration, Epic Spellcasting, Focused Spell Penetration, Forge Epic Ring, Great Intelligence Ignore Material Components, Improved Combat Casting, Tenacious Magic.
