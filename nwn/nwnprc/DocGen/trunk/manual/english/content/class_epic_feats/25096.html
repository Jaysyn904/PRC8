:: Epic Contemplative ::

Epic Contemplatives have moved beyond mere devotion into living embodiments of divine connection. Their thoughts echo the will of their deity, and their actions shape the world in subtle yet profound ways. Whether isolated in serene temples or walking amid the chaos of the world, they are guided by unshakable faith. Every breath is a prayer, every step a sacred act. In their presence, others feel the stillness of eternity, as if the divine is looking through their eyes.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Contemplative gains a bonus feat every three levels.

Special:
Spellcasting: At each class level the epic Contemplative continues to increase in divine caster level (and spells known, if applicable).
