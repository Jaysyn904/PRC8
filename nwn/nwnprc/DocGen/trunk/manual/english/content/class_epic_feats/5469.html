:: Epic Frenzied Berzerker ::

Few mortal beings can surpass the destructive capabilities of the epic frenzied berserker.

Hit Die: d12.
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2+ Intelligence modifier.
Bonus Feats: The epic frenzied berserker gains a bonus feat every three levels.

Special:
Frenzy (Ex): The epic frenzied berserker may enter a frenzy one additional time per day for every two levels gained after 9th.
Inspire Frenzy (Su): The epic frenzied berserker may use this ability one additional time per day for every two levels gained after 10th.

Epic Frenzied Berserker Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Epic Damage Reduction, Devastating Critical, Great Constitution, Great Strength, Epic Prowess, Epic Toughness, Fast Healing, Overwhelming Critical, Terrifying Rage, Thundering Rage.
