:: Epic Eye of Gruumsh ::

You are the living embodiment of Gruumsh's savage fury, a one-eyed martyr whose terrible sacrifice has unlocked epic levels of brutal power. Your scarred visage and impaired sight serve as a constant reminder of your oath, granting you a glimpse into your deity's blind rage. In the heat of battle, your ferocity and unholy devotion enable you to strike terror into the hearts of your foes, as you channel the very essence of an orcish legend.

Hit Die: d12
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Intelligence modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Eye of Gruumsh gains a bonus feat every three levels
Special:
Blinding Spittle: Your venomous saliva continues to blind your enemies. Its frequency and potency increase as you grow in power, marking you as a scourge upon the battlefield.
Ritual Scarring: The cursed marks of your sacrifice persist, evolving with each level to further empower your savage abilities.

Epic Eye of Gruumsh Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Damage Reduction, Devastating Critical, Great Constitution, Epic Prowess, Epic Toughness, Epic Weapon Focus, Fast Healing, Legendary Wrestler, Mighty Rage, Overwhelming Critical, Terrifying Rage, Thundering Rage.
