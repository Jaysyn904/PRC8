:: Epic Ghost Faced Killer ::

At epic levels, the Ghost-Faced Killer becomes a nearly unstoppable specter of death, striking from the shadows with terrifying precision. Their ghostly abilities expand, allowing more frequent use of ethereal movement and fear-based tactics. Few see them coming, and even fewer survive the encounter.

Hit Die: d8.
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Intelligence modifier.
Bonus Feats: The epic Ghost-Faced Killer gains a bonus feat every four levels.

Special:
Sudden Strike: The epic Ghost-Faced Killer's Sudden Strike damage continues to increase by +1d6 every three levels.
Ghost Step: The epic Ghost-Faced Killer continues gaining a use of their Ghost Step ability every three class levels.
Frightful Attack: The epic Ghost-Faced Killer continues gaining a use of their Frightful Attack ability every three class levels.

Epic Ghost-Faced Killer Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Devastating Critical, Epic Dodge, Epic Reputation, Epic Skill Focus, Epic Prowess, Epic Toughness, Epic Weapon Focus, Great Dexterity, Improved Sneak Attack, Lingering Damage, Overwhelming Critical, Self-Concealment, Superior Initiative.
