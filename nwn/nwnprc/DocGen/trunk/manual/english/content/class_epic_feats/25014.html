:: Epic Ironsoul Forgemaster ::

At epic levels, your mastery of meldshaping and dwarven craft reaches legendary heights. Your forge burns with divine purpose, shaping weapons and armor infused with the very essence of your soul. As a true master of your art, you stand as an unyielding guardian of dwarven heritage, wielding arms that embody both ancient tradition and unmatched power.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Ironsoul Forgemaster gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Meldshaping: At each ironsoul forgemaster level after 10th, you increase your meldshaper level, the number of soulmelds you can shape, the number of chakra binds you can create, and your essentia pool as if you had gained a level in a meldshaping class to which you belonged prior to gaining the soulcaster level. You do not, however, gain any other abilities of that class, such as new tiers of chakra binds.
