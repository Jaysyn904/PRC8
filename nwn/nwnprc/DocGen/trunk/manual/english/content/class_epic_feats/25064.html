:: Epic Acolyte of the Ego ::

You speak power into yourself, bending reality with each syllable of your own truename. As your voice grows stronger, so does your form, shifting, resonating, evolving. You are your own source of power, and none may unmake what you have named.

Hit Die: d8.
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Intelligence modifier.
Bonus Feats: The epic Acolyte of the Ego gains a bonus feat every three levels.

Special:
Morphic Cadence: The epic Acolyte of the Ego continues to pick a new Cadence every other level.
Truenaming: The epic Acolyte of the Ego continues to increase in Truenaming level.

Epic Acolyte of the Ego Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Epic Energy Resistance, Epic Fortitude, Epic Prowess, Epic Reflexes, Epic Skill Focus, Epic Toughness, Epic Reputation, Epic Will, Fast Healing, Great Intelligence, Perfect Health, Superior Initiative.
