:: Epic Arcane Duelist ::

An Epic Arcane Duelist becomes an untouchable master of deception and finesse, wielding illusion and swordplay in perfect harmony. Their chosen weapon strikes with uncanny precision, their defenses seem impenetrable, and they bewilder foes with dazzling speed and illusions, making them nearly impossible to best in combat.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Intelligence modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Arcane Duelist gains a bonus feat every four levels.

Special:
Enchant Chosen Weapon: The epic Arcane Duelist's Chosen Weapon bonus continues to increase by +1 every other level.
Blur - The epic Arcane Duelist receives another use of their Blur ability every level.
False Keeness - The epic Arcane Duelist receives another use of their Blur ability every level.
Flurry of Swords - The epic Arcane Duelist receives another use of their Flurry of Swords ability every level.

Epic Arcane Duelist Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Damage Reduction, Devastating Critical, Energy Resistance, Epic Fortitude, Epic Leadership, Epic Prowess, Epic Reflexes, Epic Toughness, Epic Weapon Focus, Epic Will, Great Charisma, Great Intelligence, Improved Combat Casting, Improved Whirlwind Attack, Legendary Commander, Overwhelming Critical, Perfect Two-Weapon Fighting, Superior Initiative, Two-Weapon Rend.
