:: Epic Serene Guardian ::

You are a transcendent guardian of the Luminous Order, channeling disciplined meditation into devastating resonance with every strike.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2+ Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic serene guardian gains a bonus feat every other level

Epic Serene Guardian Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Damage Reduction, Devastating Critical, Distant Shot, Epic Endurance, Epic Leadership, Epic Prowess, Energy Resistance, Epic Toughness, Epic Weapon Focus, Epic Weapon Specialization, Improved Combat Reflexes, Improved Manyshot, Improved Stunning Fist, Improved Whirlwind Attack, Legendary Commander, Legendary Wrestler, Overwhelming Critical, Perfect Two-Weapon Fighting, Spellcasting Harrier, Superior Initiative, Two-Weapon Rend. In addition to the feats on this list, the epic serene guardian may treat any feat designated as a fighter bonus feat, but not listed here, as being on his or her bonus feat list.
