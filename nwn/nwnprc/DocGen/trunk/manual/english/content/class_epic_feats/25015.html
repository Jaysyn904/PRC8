:: Epic Stormlord ::

At epic levels, you become a living tempest, a walking manifestation of Talos? chaotic fury. Thunder and lightning answer your call, and your mere presence heralds destruction. Wielding your spear like the storm itself, you lay waste to all who stand before you, leaving only ruin in your wake.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Stormlord gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Spellcasting: The epic Stormlord continues to increase in caster level (and spells known, if applicable).

Epic Stormlord Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Automatic Quicken Spell, Automatic Silent Spell, Automatic Still Spell, Blinding Speed, Energy Resistance, Epic Fortitude, Epic Prowess, Epic Spell Focus, Epic Spell Penetration, Epic Spellcasting, Epic Toughness, Epic Weapon Focus (any spear or javelin), Ignore Material Components, Improved Combat Casting, Planar Turning, Tenacious Magic, Undead Mastery, Zone of Animation.
