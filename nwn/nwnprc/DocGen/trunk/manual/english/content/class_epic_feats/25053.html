:: Epic Noctumancer ::

Epic Noctumancers are master weavers of arcane and shadow threads, effortlessly unraveling the magic of others while reinforcing their own with stolen power. With eyes that pierce the weave and shroud alike, they bend hostile spells into fuel for their next incantation.

Hit Die: d4
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Noctumancer gains a bonus feat every five levels.

Special:
Innate Counterspell: The epic Noctumancer continues to gain an Innate Counterspell use per day for every three class levels past 8th lvl.
Mysteries: The epic Noctumancer continues to increase in mystery caster level (and mysteries known, if applicable).
Spellcasting: The epic Noctumancer continues to increase in caster level (and spells known, if applicable).

Epic Noctumancer Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Automatic Quicken Spell, Automatic Silent Spell, Automatic Still Spell, Blinding Speed, Epic Energy Resistance, Epic Spell Focus, Epic Spell Penetration, Epic Spellcasting, Favored Mystery, Focused Spell Penetration, Great Charisma, Great Intelligence, Greater Path Focus, Ignore Material Components, Improved Combat Casting, Nocturnal Caster, Path Focus, Shadow Cast, Superior Initiative, Tenacious Magic, and any metashadow feat.
