:: Epic Runescarred Berserker ::

Epic Runescarred Berserkers are living weapons of wrath and arcane resilience. Their rune-covered flesh blazes with potent magic, their fury eclipsing mortal limits. As chosen champions of Rashemen, they channel raw pain into devastating power-each scar a rune, each scream a spell, each rage an unstoppable storm.

Hit Die: d10.
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Intelligence modifier.
Bonus Feats: The epic Runescarred Berserker gains a bonus feat every four levels.

Special:
Runescar Progression: The epic Runescarred Berzerker's runescars continue to advance in caster level.
Damage Reduction: The epic Runescarred Berzerker continues to gain +1 DR every 3 class levels.
Ritual Scarring : The epic Runescarred Berzerker continues to gain +1 Natural AC every 3 class levels.
