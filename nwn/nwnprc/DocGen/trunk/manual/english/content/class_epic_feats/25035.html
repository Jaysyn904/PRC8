:: Epic Soldier of Light ::

Steeled in the fires of righteousness, the Epic Soldier of Light becomes a beacon of divine power, radiating positive energy that shatters darkness and evil wherever it lurks. These champions stand unwavering against the forces of undeath and corruption, their faith and blade striking with celestial fury.

Hit Die: d10
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Soldier of Light gains a bonus feat every three levels after 10th.

CLASS FEATURES:

Level
13: Healing Domain - Healing spells are empowered.
16: Maximize Turn Undead - When you turn undead, your turn damage is multiplied by 2 (Doesn't stack with Empower Turning).
19: Fast Healing 3 - Regenerate 3 HP/round.
22: Supreme Positive Energy Burst: Now you only spend 1 turn attempt when you use Positive Energy Burst.

Epic Soldier of Light Bonus Feats List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Devastating Critical, Epic Divine Might, Epic Divine Shield, Epic Divine Resistance, Epic Divine Vigor, Epic Leadership, Epic Prowess, Epic Reputation, Epic Toughness, Epic Weapon Focus, Great Smiting, Great Wisdom, Legendary Commander, Overwhelming Critical, Perfect Health, Planar Turning.
