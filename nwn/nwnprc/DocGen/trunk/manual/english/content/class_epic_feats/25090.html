:: Epic Pyrokineticist ::

At epic levels, the Pyrokineticist becomes the living embodiment of raw elemental force. Whether wielding searing flame, freezing cold, corrosive acid, or crackling lightning, they channel destructive energy with deadly precision and flair. Their chosen element shapes every strike, movement, and breath unleashing devastating touch attacks, elemental lashes, and overwhelming bursts of power. Masters of heat, chill, corrosion, or storm, these kineticists don't just manipulate energy, they become it.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Pyrokineticist gains a bonus feat every three levels.

Epic Pyrokineticist Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Epic Damage Reduction, Epic Energy Resistance, Epic Fortitude, Epic Prowess, Epic Reflexes, Epic Reputation, Epic Toughness, Epic Weapon Focus, Epic Will, Fast Healing, Great Charisma, Epic Psionic Feats, Superior Initiative.
