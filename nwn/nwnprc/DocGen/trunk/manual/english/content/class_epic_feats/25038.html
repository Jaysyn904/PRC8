:: Epic Initiate of Draconic Mysteries ::

The Epic Initiate of Draconic Mysteries ascends beyond mortal limits, embodying the ancient power of dragons. Their unarmed strikes can rend steel, their senses pierce the darkest shadows, and their very presence instills awe and fear.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Initiate of Draconic Mysteries gains a bonus feat every five levels

Special:
Claw of the Dragon: The epic Initiate of Draconic Mysteries' unarmed strike continues to gain +1 enhancement every 3 class levels.

Epic Initiate of Draconic Mysteries Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Epic Damage Reduction, Epic Energy Resistance, Epic Prowess, Epic Reputation, Epic Self Concealment, Epic Toughness, Fast Healing, Great Charisma, Great Wisdom, Improved Ki Strike, Improved Spell Resistance, Improved Stunning Fist, Perfect Health, Superior Initiative.
