:: Dimensional Jaunt ::

Type of Feat: Reserve
Prerequisite: Ability to cast 4th-level spells
Benefit: As long as you have a teleportation spell of 4th level or higher available to cast, you can spend a standard action to teleport yourself and carried objects up to your heavy load a distance of 5 feet per level of the highest-level teleportation spell you have available to cast. You can teleport only to a location that you can see (including one you are currently scrying). You can't bring along another creature (except for a familiar). As a secondary benefit, you gain a +1 competence bonus to your caster level when casting teleportation spells.
Use: Activated
