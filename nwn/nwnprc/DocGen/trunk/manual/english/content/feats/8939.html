:: Double Chakra - Brow ::

Type of Feat: Incarnum
Prerequisite: Meldshaper level 9th
Specifics: When this feat is selected, choose a chakra to which you can bind soulmelds. Two of your shaped soulmelds can occupy (and be bound to) that chakra simultaneously. This counts as two chakra binds.
Use: Automatic

Required For the Following Feats:

Rebind Soulmeld - Brow
