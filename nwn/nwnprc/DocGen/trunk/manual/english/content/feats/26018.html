:: Pain Touch ::

Type of Feat: General

You cause intense pain in an opponent with a successful stunning attack.

Prerequisite:Stunning Fist (or monk's stunning attack) , WIS 15, base attack bonus +2
Specifics:Victims of a successful stunning attack are subject to such debilitating pain that they are nauseated for 1 round following the round they are stunned. Creatures that are immune to stunning attacks are also immune to the effect of this feat, as are any creatures that are more than one size category larger than the feat user.
Use: Automatic.

Requires All of These Feats:

Stunning Fist
