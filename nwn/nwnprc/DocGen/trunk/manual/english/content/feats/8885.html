:: Midnight Dodge ::

Type of Feat: Incarnum
Prerequisite: Con 13, Dex 13
Specifics: Once per day, you can invest essentia in this feat. You receive a dodge bonus to Armor Class equal to the invested essentia. You gain 1 point of essentia.
Use: Selected

Required For the Following Feats:

Mobility
Quick Staff
Sidestep Charge
Spring Attack
