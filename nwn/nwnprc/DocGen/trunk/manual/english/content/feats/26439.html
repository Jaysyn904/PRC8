:: Selunite Scent ::

A Silverstar of 6th level or higher who is a werewolf may select this feat as a normal feat choice. It grants Keen Senses and a +4 bonus to Spot, Listen, and Search while she remains a werewolf. The skill bonus does not stack with Scent.

Requires All of These Feats:

Selunite Lycanthrope
Shapechanger
