:: Holy Warrior ::

Type of Feat: Reserve
Prerequisite: Ability to cast 4th-level spells, access to the War domain
Benefit: As long as you have a 4th-level or higher War domain spell available to cast, you gain a bonus on your weapon damage rolls equal to the level of the highest-level War spell you have available to cast. As a secondary benefit, you gain a +1 competence bonus to your caster level when casting force spells.
Use: Passive

Requires One of These Feats:

Bonus Domain: War
