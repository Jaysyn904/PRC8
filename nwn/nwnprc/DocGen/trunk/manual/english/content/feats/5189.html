:: Bear Fang ::

Type of Feat: General
Prerequisite: Power Attack, Two-Weapon Fighting, Weapon Focus (dagger), Weapon Focus (battle axe, hand axe, or dwarven waraxe), Str 15
Benefit: If you hit a creature twice in the same round, you immediately attempt to start a grapple as a free action without provoking an attack of opportunity. No touch attack is required. If you succeed on your grapple attempt, you unequip your axe, but you immediately gain an additional attack against your grappled foe with your dagger at your highest base attack bonus (with the normal -4 penalty for attacking in a grapple).
Use: Automatic

Requires All of These Feats:

Power Attack
Two-Weapon Fighting
