:: Open Least Chakra - Crown ::

Type of Feat: Incarnum
Prerequisite: Con 13, character level 6th
Specifics: When this feat is selected, choose one of the following chakras: crown, feet, or hands. You can now bind a soulmeld to that chakra. In addition, you gain a minor benefit, depending on the chakra chosen:
Crown: +1 insight bonus on Will saves.
Feet: +1 insight bonus on Balance and Move Silently checks.
Hands: +1 insight bonus on Climb and Jump checks.
Use: Automatic
