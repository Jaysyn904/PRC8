:: Invisible Needle ::

Type of Feat: Reserve
Prerequisite: Ability to cast 3rd-level spells
Benefit: As long as you have a force spell of 3rd level or higher available to cast, you can use a standard action to hurl a tiny needle-shaped projectile created from pure force. This attack requires a successful ranged attack roll (not a ranged touch attack), and the dart has a range of 5 feet per level of the force spell. The needle deals 1d4 points of damage per level of the highest-level force spell you have available. Because it is composed of force, the needle can strike incorporeal creatures. As a secondary benefit, you gain a +1 competence bonus to your caster level when casting force spells.
Use: Activated
