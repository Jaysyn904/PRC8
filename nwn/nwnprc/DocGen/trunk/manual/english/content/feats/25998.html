:: Charming the Arrow ::

Type of Feat: General
Prerequisite: Fey
Specifics: You may use your Charisma modifier instead of your Dexterity modifier on attack rolls when using a bow or crossbow.
Use: Automatic
Special: A fighter can select Charming the Arrow as one of his fighter bonus feats.

Requires One of These Feats:

Fey
Fey metamorphosis
