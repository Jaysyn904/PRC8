:: Silverstar ::


(PRESTIGE CLASS)
Silverstars are wandering champions of Selune who defend the innocent, expose darkness, and oppose evil shapechangers.

- Hit Die: d8.
- Base Attack Bonus: Medium.
- High Saves: Fortitude and Will.
- Skill Points: 2 + Intelligence modifier.
- Spellcasting: Every Silverstar level advances the character's primary divine spellcasting class.

REQUIREMENTS:

Alignment: Chaotic Good
Base Attack Bonus: +4
Skills: Sense Motive 2 ranks, Spot 2 ranks
Feats: Blind-Fight, Dodge, Mobility, Spring Attack
Spells: Able to cast 2nd-level divine spells
Special: A Cleric must possess the Moon domain.

CLASS FEATURES:

Level
1: Moon Spells - Cast Moon domain spells using spell slots of the same level from the divine spellcasting class advanced by Silverstar.
2: Low-Light Vision.
3: Moon's Hand - A wielded heavy mace gains +1 enhancement and +1d6 electrical damage.
4: Tears of Selune - Dancing Lights at will.
5: Prophet's Sight - True Seeing once per rest.
6: Selunite Lycanthrope - A Silverstar who is a werewolf gains control over her transformations. Does not grant lycanthropy.
Improved Moon's Hand - The heavy mace's enhancement increases to +2, retaining +1d6 electrical damage.
7: Moonshield - +2 on saving throws against Enchantment, Illusion, Necromancy, and Darkness spells.
8: Prophet's Sight - Two uses per rest.
9: Greater Moon's Hand - Adds an electrical burst, checked by a separate threat roll on each successful hit.
10: Moonfire - Once per rest.

EPIC PROGRESSION:

Silverstar levels 11-30 are available from character level 21. Moon's Hand improves to +3 at class level 15, +4 at 20, +5 at 25, and +6 at 30. Prophet's Sight increases to 3 uses per rest at class level 16, 4 at 23, and 5 at 30. Gain an epic bonus feat at class levels 14, 18, 22, 26, and 30. Divine spellcasting continues to advance.
