:: Warforged Juggernaut ::


(PRESTIGE CLASS)
Some believe that the ultimate expression of the warforged experiment is the juggernaut, a path that allows warforged to become more and more like true constructs and less like their living creators. As a machine of war, the juggernaut is among the best at dealing damage and sustaining punishment. Some warforged refuse to take up the mantle of juggernaut because it accentuates their construct heritage at the cost of their living aspects. Nonetheless, for a warforged who wants to become an engine of destruction, the juggernaut prestige class is the best road by which to get there.

- Hit Die: d12
- Proficiencies: A Warforged Juggernaut gains no additional proficiencies
- Skill Points: 2+ Int Modifier.

Race: Warforged
Feats: Adamantine Body, Improved Bull Rush, Power Attack
Base Attack Bonus: +5
