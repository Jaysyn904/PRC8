:: Bind Zuriel ::

Type of Feat: Class / Epic
Prerequisite: Effective Binder level 21st, ability to bind 8th-level vestiges, Lore 27 ranks
Specifics: You can bind the epic vestige Zuriel, the Bronze God (DC 43). A just deity of war who sacrificed his life and divinity defending his people against a githyanki invasion, Zuriel grants Bronze Body, Forceful Speed of Thought, Pact Fortification, and Ultimate Sacrifice.
Use: Automatic.

Requires All of These Feats:

Soul Binding

Required For the Following Feats:

Bind Additional Epic Vestige I
Favored Vestige Zuriel
