:: Bind Additional Epic Vestige I ::

Type of Feat: Class / Epic
Prerequisite: Effective Binder level 21st, ability to bind at least two epic vestiges.
Specifics: You can bind one additional epic vestige simultaneously (up to two with I, three with II, and all four with III). Bound epic vestiges still count toward your total bound vestige limit.
Use: Automatic.

Requires All of These Feats:

Soul Binding

Requires One of These Feats:

Bind Amun-her Khepeshef
Bind Gaia
Bind Tkhaluuljin
Bind Zuriel

Feat Replaced By:

Bind Additional Epic Vestige II

Required For the Following Feats:

Bind Additional Epic Vestige II
