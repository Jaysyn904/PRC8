:: Bind Additional Vestige IV ::

Type of Feat: Class / Epic
Prerequisite: Effective Binder level 21st, ability to bind 8th-level vestiges.
Specifics: You can bind one additional nonepic vestige simultaneously, increasing the maximum number of vestiges you can have bound at once by 1. You may take this feat up to ten times; its effects stack.
Use: Automatic.

Bind Additional Vestige III

Feat Replaced By:

Bind Additional Vestige V
