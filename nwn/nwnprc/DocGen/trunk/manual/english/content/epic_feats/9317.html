:: Bind Amun-her Khepeshef ::

Type of Feat: Class / Epic
Prerequisite: Effective Binder level 21st, ability to bind 8th-level vestiges, Lore 29 ranks
Specifics: You can bind the epic vestige Amun-her Khepeshef, the Desecrated Scion (DC 47). Once the firstborn heir to his kingdom, Amun-her Khepeshef represents the collective souls of the empire's slain firstborn sons and grants Burst of Life, Delay Death, Firstborn Guardian, and Scion of War.
Use: Automatic.

Requires All of These Feats:

Soul Binding

Required For the Following Feats:

Bind Additional Epic Vestige I
Favored Vestige Amun-her Khepeshef
