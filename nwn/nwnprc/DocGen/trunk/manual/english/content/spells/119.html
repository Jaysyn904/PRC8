:: Minor Globe of Invulnerability ::


Caster Level(s): Wizard/Sorcerer 4
Innate Level: 4
School: Abjuration
Descriptor(s):
Component(s): Verbal, Somatic
Range: Personal
Area of Effect / Target: Caster
Duration: 1 Round / Level
Additional Counter Spells:
Save: Harmless
