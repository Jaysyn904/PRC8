:: Blink ::


Level: Brd 3, Src/Wiz 3
Components: V, S
Casting Time: 1 standard action
Range: Personal
Target: You
Duration: 1 round/level

You bounce between the ethereal and the material plane. This grants you a 50% miss chance on attacks, a 20% chance to miss attacks you make, a 20% chance to fail at casting any spells, +2 to attack, and a 50% chance for spells targeted at you to fail.
