:: Baleful Transposition ::


Caster Level(s): Sorc/Wiz 2
Innate Level: 2
School: Conjuration
Descriptor(s):
Component(s): Verbal
Range: Medium
Area of Effect / Target: Single
Duration: Instant
Additional Counter Spells:
