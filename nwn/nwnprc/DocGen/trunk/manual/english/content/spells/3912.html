:: Force Orb ::


Caster Level(s): Sorc/Wiz 4
Evocation [Force]
Component(s): Verbal, Somatic
Range: Medium
Area of Effect / Target: Single.
Duration: Instant
Save: Fort half or Reflex half, see text
Spell Resistance: Yes

The caster targets a single creature with a force orb. The orb deals 1d6/lvl dmg (max 10d6). If the orb misses, it explodes, dealing two damage per dice in a 10 foot area, Reflex half.
