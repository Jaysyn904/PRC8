:: Raise Dead ::


Caster Level(s): Cleric 5
Innate Level: 5
School: Conjuration
Descriptor(s):
Component(s): Verbal, Somatic
Range: Touch
Area of Effect / Target: Single
Duration: Instant
Additional Counter Spells:
Save: Harmless

Returns one target corpse to life. The raised character returns to life with only 1 Hit Point.
