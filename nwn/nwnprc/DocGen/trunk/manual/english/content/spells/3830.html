:: Hallow ::


Hallow
Level: Clr 5, Drd 5
Components: V,S
Range: Touch
Area: 40-ft. radius emanating from the touched point
Duration: 24 hrs
Saving Throw: No
Spell Resistance: No

The area is guarded by a magic circle against evil effect. Also, all Charisma checks made to turn undead gain a +4 sacred bonus, and Charisma checks to command undead take a -4 penalty.
