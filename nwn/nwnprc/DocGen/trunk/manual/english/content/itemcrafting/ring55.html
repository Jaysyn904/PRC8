:: Ring of Epic Divinity V ::


Like the ring of divinity, this ring comes in a variety of types useful only to divine spellcasters. The wearer's divine spells per day are doubled for one particular spell level. An epic ring of divinity V doubles 5th-level spells, an epic ring of divinity VI doubles 6th-level spells, an epic ring of divinity VII doubles 7th-level spells, an epic ring of divinity VIII doubles 8th-level spells, and an epic ring of divinity IX doubles 9th-level spells. Bonus spells from high ability scores, school specialization, or any other source are not doubled.

Prerequisites: Forge Ring, Forge Epic Ring, miracle
