:: Choose a Breath Effect ::


At 2nd, 5th, 10th, 12th, 15th, and 20th levels you can choose a breath effect, which allows you to modify your breath weapon's damage type, area, or even replace the damage with another effect. Some only work with cones, while others only work with lines, and those of the same type cannot stack.
