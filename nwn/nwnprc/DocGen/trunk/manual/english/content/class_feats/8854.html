:: Weapon Bond ::

Type of Feat: Class
Prerequisite: Ironsoul Forgemaster 9
Specifics: At 9th level, you create a special bond with any weapon that you craft. This bond allows you to invest essentia in the weapon as if it were a soulmeld. Doing so grants you a bonus equal to twice the invested essentia on your damage rolls. If you have at least 1 point of essentia invested in the weapon, it also dazes any living opponent you strike with it for 1 round (Fortitude negates, DC 10 + invested essentia + Con modifier). You gain this ability only while using the weapon. If you unequip the weapon, you lose the soulmeld.
Use: Selected
