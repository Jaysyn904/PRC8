Amun-her Khepeshef, the Desecrated Scion

Once the firstborn heir to his people's kingdom, Amun-her Khepeshef represents the souls of the firstborn sons of an ancient empire, whose lives were tragically extinguished by the god of their slaves. When grave robbers and rival invaders desecrated their tombs and destroyed the sanctity of their afterlife, the furious spirits appealed to the god of the dead to banish them beyond space and time as a single bound entity. Amun-her Khepeshef grants the ability to continue to act while dying, protection from death effects and the attacks of undead, bonuses on combat maneuvers in battle, and the ability to release a powerful burst of positive energy.

Vestige Level: 9th (Epic)
Binding DC: 47
Special Requirement: You cannot bind Amun-her Khepeshef if you are currently suffering from any negative levels or ability damage, and only living creatures can bind him.

Manifestation: When Amun-her Khepeshef manifests, a handsome tanned male warrior appears in a stone sarcophagus, his body resplendent in gold and gems. Suddenly, the warrior bolts upright and wails a scream of pain that sounds like the collective voices of men and boys. His body explodes into overlapping images of thousands of men and male children with jaundiced faces and sunken cheeks, who wail in unison before merging back into the warrior as he lies still and dead once more.

Sign: You take on the look of the dead: jaundiced skin, sunken cheeks, bags under the eyes, and stiff movements, exuding a faint odor of decay and preservative natron salts.

Influence: You cannot abide the presence of undead, necromancers, and death spells and effects. You seek any opportunity to slay undead, and you refuse to work with anyone who uses necromancy and death effects.

Granted Abilities:
While bound to Amun-her Khepeshef, you can release a powerful burst of positive energy that heals the living and harms the dead, function even at the brink of death, gain immunity to death effects and undead attacks (and extend this protection to allies), and project an aura that enhances battle tactics.

Burst of Life: As a standard action, you can release a 30-foot burst of positive energy centered on yourself. All living allies in the area (including you) receive a 1-round 50% concealment aura along with the effects of a heal spell (restoring up to 250 hit points and removing adverse conditions; Warforged receive half healing plus the full restorative scrub, Tomb-Tainted Soul and Acererak's healing are respected, and Frenzied Berserker nonlethal damage debt is cleared) and a greater restoration spell (removing ability drain, negative levels including Energy Ebb, fatigue, exhaustion, Ghoul Gauntlet, Touch of Juiblex, and afflicted lycanthropy without XP cost). Non-friendly undead in the area take positive energy damage from the heal effect (10 points per effective binder level, maximum 250; Will save for half, leaving at least 1d4 hit points). Once you have used this ability, you cannot do so again for 5 rounds.

Delay Death: For a total of 1 round per effective binder level per day (split across multiple occurrences as needed), you do not die from hit point loss when reduced to 1 hit point or lower, continuing to act normally without disability. Daily Delay Death rounds are only expended when no other persistent immortality effect (such as Gaia's unlocked Planetary Healing, Deathless Frenzy, or Immortal Fortitude) is already preventing your death at 1 hit point, and the countdown pauses whenever you are healed above 1 hit point. Once your daily rounds are exhausted, this protection ends until you rest.

Firstborn Guardian: You gain constant immunity to fatigue, exhaustion, ability damage and drain, energy drain, death spells, magical death effects, negative energy effects, and undead-inflicted fear, disease, paralysis, and poison. In addition, you gain Ultravision and the Blind-Fight feat, all weapons and natural attacks you wield gain the ghost touch quality (bypassing incorporeal miss chance and restoring the 50% physical weapon damage normally lost to incorporeal immunity), and you gain a +4 deflection bonus to AC against undead. As a swift action, you can extend all of these protections to up to 1 ally per 5 effective binder levels within 30 feet for 1 round per effective binder level (5-round cooldown).

Scion of War: You project a 60-foot aura that enhances the battle prowess of you and your allies within 60 feet, granting twice your Charisma modifier as a bonus on bull rush, disarm, grapple, overrun, sunder, and trip checks.
