:: Spellfire (Blasting) ::

Type of Feat: Class
Prerequisite: Knight of the Weave 10th
Benefit: You can burn a single arcane spell as a standard action to make a ranged touch attack dealing 1d6 points of spellfire damage per spell level expended, Reflex DC 20 half. Spellfire damage is half fire damage and half magical damage.
Use: Selected
