:: Aspect of the Destroyer ::

Type of Feat: Race
Prerequisite: Killoren
Benefit: You gain a smite attack that adds your Charisma to attack and your level to damage. This smite attack works only against aberrations, constructs, humanoids, oozes, outsiders, and undead. You may smite a number of times equal to your charisma modifier. You cannot have multiple aspects at one time.
Use: Selected

Required For the Following Feats:

Killoren Destroyer
