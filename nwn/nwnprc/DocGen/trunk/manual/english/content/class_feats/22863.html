:: Knock Silly ::

Type of Feat: Legacy
Prerequisite: Least Legacy Ritual
Benefit: At 6th level and higher, when you deal damage to an opponent with Bullybasher's Gauntlets, that opponent is affected as if by the touch of idiocy spell. You must decide whether or not to use this ability before making the attack roll, and if you miss, the attempt is wasted for the day. This ability is usable two times per day. Caster level 5th.
Use: Selected
