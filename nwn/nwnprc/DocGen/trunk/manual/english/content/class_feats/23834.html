:: Armor of Shadow ::

Type of Feat: Shadowcasting
Prerequisite: Shadowsmith 4
Specifics: Starting at 4th level, you can take a standard action to surround yourself in hardened shadow. This ability grants you a +2 deflection bonus to AC. Armor of shadow lasts for 10 minutes per class level, and is usable a number of times per day equal to one-half your class level. At 7th level, the deflection bonus increases to +4. At 10th level, you quicken your armor of shadow, activating it as a swift action.
Use: Selected.

Feat Replaced By:

Armor of Shadow
