:: Alternate Form (Hybrid) ::

Type of Feat: Werewolf Template
Specifics: Assume bipedal hybrid form or return to humanoid form as a standard action. A D&D 3.0 template werewolf gains +2 Strength, +4 Dexterity, +4 Constitution, +4 total natural armor (the wolf's +2, increased by the template's +2), scent, bite and trip, and the werewolf's form-dependent feats and skills. The tabletop DR 15/silver is encoded as DR 15/+1, the closest stock NWN property. Assuming hybrid form restores hit points as one day of rest. Legacy 4.947 werewolf-class characters retain their staged class form.

An afflicted werewolf must be aware of the curse and succeed on a Control Shape check. PRC 4.947 has no Control Shape skill row, so this implementation uses a Wisdom check at the original D&D 3.0 DC.
