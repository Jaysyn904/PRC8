:: Gaia - Elemental Fortitude ::

Gaia, Soul of the Land

Once the living nature goddess of a lost continent and the body of that land itself, Gaia was the patron of all living creatures and the defender of sacred life. When the gargantuan alien entity Tkhaluuljin attacked and swallowed her continent whole, Gaia continued to fight from within the beast's stomach alongside her surviving followers, mortally wounding her captor so that it plummeted into the ocean and destroyed them both. Gaia grants her summoners the ability to feel the pulse of the land, giving them potent advantages against unnatural creatures, heightened earthly senses, immunity to elemental forces, the voice of nature, and regeneration.

Vestige Level: 9th (Epic)
Binding DC: 39
Special Requirement: You must summon Gaia outdoors and above ground. She does not answer your call if you cannot see the sky.

Manifestation: When Gaia manifests, a large green sphere appears in the sky, its landmasses and oceans growing brighter until it explodes in a ball of fire and plasma. In the afterimage of the explosion, the silhouette of a beautiful green-skinned woman appears briefly before leaving behind an ethereal female figure.

Sign: The hair on your face, head, and body takes on a greenish hue, as do your eyes and skin.

Influence: Never allow cruelty toward living creatures to occur in your presence. Seek out every opportunity to destroy aberrations, constructs, oozes, and undead.

Granted Abilities:
While bound to Gaia, you gain potent combat advantages against unnatural creatures, a sensory connection to the land, the voice to calm living creatures, immunity to a chosen element, and wound regeneration.

Earth's Fury: Your attacks and powers are more potent against aberrations, constructs, oozes, and undead. Against creatures of these types, all of your spells, binding abilities, and racial spell-like abilities gain a +4 bonus to their saving throw DCs, both your scripted and standard attacks (via weapon OnHit hooks) bypass their immunity to critical hits, sneak attacks, and precision damage, you automatically confirm critical threats against them, and you gain a +4 bonus to AC and saving throws against their attacks.

Earthsense: You gain potent senses derived from your connection to the land, granting Ultravision, True Seeing, the Track feat, and Scent (+10 competence bonus on Spot and Search checks).

Earth's Voice: You gain a +10 bonus on Persuade and Animal Empathy checks (representing Polyglot, speak with plants, and speak with animals) and project a persistent 30-foot aura (stays active until toggled off; 5-round cooldown after suppressing) in which any living non-friendly creature that hears your voice must succeed on a Will save each round or be charmed (scaled per PRC charm conventions to Daze against player characters and Charm against non-player creatures) for 1 round per effective binder level.

Elemental Fortitude: You gain 100% immunity to one chosen element (Acid, Cold, Electricity, or Fire; defaults to Acid on binding). Once every 5 rounds, you can switch your elemental immunity to a different element via your radial menu.

Planetary Healing: Living creatures (including Warforged, but excluding undead and non-living constructs) gain regeneration equal to 1 point per 4 effective binder levels (you cannot be killed by hit point damage unless dealt fire or acid damage, which suppresses your regeneration for 1 round; if reduced to 1 hit point by non-fire/acid damage, you are knocked down for 1 round while regenerating). As a standard action, you can share your regeneration 1 point at a time with touched living allies (by default, you and every living ally holding at least 1 shared point gain lethal-damage immunity overcome by fire or acid; configurable via PRC_GAIA_PLANETARY_HEALING_NERF), and you can recall all shared points to yourself at any time as an immediate action.
