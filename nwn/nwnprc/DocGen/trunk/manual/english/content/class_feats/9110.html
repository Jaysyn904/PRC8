Tkhaluuljin, the Cephalopocalypse

A gargantuan alien squidlike entity, Tkhaluuljin traveled the planes devouring whatever blocked its path. In the language of the gith, its name translates roughly as "living armageddon." Emerging from a planar rift or black hole as an embodiment of pure, insatiable hunger rather than malice, Tkhaluuljin met its end when it swallowed the living continent of Gaia and was torn apart from within, exploding in a cataclysmic detonation. When you bind Tkhaluuljin, you project an aura of madness, move with alien flight, unleash mind-shattering blasts, and summon a sphere of annihilation.

Vestige Level: 9th (Epic)
Binding DC: 49
Special Requirement: None

Manifestation: When Tkhaluuljin manifests, a black rift opens in the air, absorbing all light and pulling matter toward the portal. With a strange slurping sound and a flash of dark magenta light, the tentacle-mawed head and bulbous translucent body of an enormous squidlike creature emerge before the rift hurtles into you, imbuing you with the essence of destruction.

Sign: Your veins appear spidery and more pronounced, emitting a pulsing magenta light.

Influence: You can never pass up the opportunity to eat, and you must consume five times the amount of food you would normally eat when not bound to Tkhaluuljin.

Granted Abilities:
While bound to Tkhaluuljin, you can project an aura of madness, fly with alien speed and grace, release a cone of stunning psychic energy, and call forth a sphere of annihilation.

Aura of Madness: You project a persistent 10-foot aura of alien madness (stays active until toggled off; 5-round cooldown after suppressing). All living creatures (other than you, including allies unless friendly fire is disabled) within 10 feet must succeed on a Will save or be confused for 1 round per 5 effective binder levels (respecting PRC confusion immunities including Dahlver-Nar's Mad Soul and Zceryll's Alien Mind); a creature that successfully saves is immune to your Aura of Madness for 24 hours (though still vulnerable to another binder's Aura of Madness).

Flight of the Alien: You fly with bizarre, undulating alien grace at 150-foot base speed (+99% movement speed increase, exempt from medium/heavy armor speed penalties), gaining true flight (PRCIsFlying and unlimited PRC Jump), Freedom of Movement, a +20 bonus on Jump checks, and the Dodge, Mobility, and Spring Attack bonus feats.

Mind Blast: As a standard action, you can release a 120-foot cone mind blast. Anyone caught in the cone (other than you, including allies unless friendly fire is disabled) must succeed on a Will save or be stunned for 12 rounds. Once you have used this ability, you must wait 5 rounds before using it again.

Sphere of Annihilation: As a full-round action, you can summon a sphere of annihilation within control range (40 ft + 10 ft per effective binder level) for up to 1 round per effective binder level per day (split across multiple uses however you wish). Moving and directing the sphere rolls a DC 30 control check (1d20 + effective binder level + the higher of your Charisma or Intelligence modifier, doubled if you possess a Talisman of the Sphere; speed 10 ft + 5 ft per 5 points over DC 30, or slides 10 ft toward you on a failed check). Any creature contacted by the sphere is instantly annihilated (or dealt 40d6 damage with a Fortitude save for 5d6 and no daily round limit when PRC_TKHAL_SPHERE_DISINTEGRATE is enabled; you are immune to a sphere you summon), and any stationary area-of-effect spell or Zuriel Wall of Force / Forcecage contacted by the sphere is destroyed while mobile creature auras are preserved. When no enemies are present, the sphere returns to you and automatically dismisses itself out of combat (or follows you when PRC_TKHAL_SPHERE_DISINTEGRATE is enabled). Once dismissed or exhausted, you must wait 5 rounds before summoning it again.
