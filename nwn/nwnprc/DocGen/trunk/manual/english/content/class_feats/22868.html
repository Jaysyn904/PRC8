:: Axe Casting ::

Type of Feat: Legacy
Prerequisite: Least Legacy Ritual
Benefit: Starting at 7th level, once per day as an immediate action, you can drive the blade of Lorestealer into a single scroll containing a 3rd-level or lower spell. Once you have done this, you can then cast the scroll at any time. Scrolls still must be deciphered before this feature functions on them. At 13th level and higher, this ability works on scrolls containing spells of 6th level or lower. Starting at 16th level, you can use this ability two times per day.
Use: Selected
