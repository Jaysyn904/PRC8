:: Fury ::

Type of Feat: Racial
Prerequisite: Wildren
Specifics: Once per day, a wildren who has taken at least 1 point of damage can choose to enter a brief state of rage-like fury at the beginning of her next turn. In this state, a wildren gains +4 to Strength and -2 to Armor Class. The fury lasts for 1 round, and a wildren cannot end her fury voluntarily. The effect of this ability stacks with similar effects (such as the barbarian's rage class feature).
Use: Selected
