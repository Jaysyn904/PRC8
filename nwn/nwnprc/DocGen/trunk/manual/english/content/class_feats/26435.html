:: Greater Moon's Hand ::

At 9th level, Moon's Hand retains its +2 enhancement bonus and +1d6 electrical damage and gains an electrical burst. Each successful hit makes a separate roll against the mace's modified critical threat range. A successful roll deals an additional 1d10 electrical damage for a x2 critical multiplier, 2d10 for x3, and so on. Creatures immune to critical hits are immune to this burst.
