Zuriel, the Bronze God

Zuriel was a just and stalwart deity of reasoned war who spoke for and defended the people of his land. When githyanki armies invaded through reawakened planar portals and slew his fellow gods, Zuriel stood as the last defense between innocent citizens and enslavement to the Lich Queen. He sacrificed his divinity and his life to erect a barrier against the invaders, but was struck down during the ritual as his life force dissipated into the ether. Zuriel grants you the ability to augment your fighting prowess in bronze armor, employ quickened force effects, double your pact augmentations, and absorb damage suffered by allies.

Vestige Level: 9th (Epic)
Binding DC: 43
Special Requirement: You cannot bind Zuriel if you are evil-aligned.

Manifestation: When Zuriel manifests, a 10-foot-tall, bronze-skinned male figure with an athletic physique stands before you in proud dignity. Suddenly, he looks up at visions of githyanki riding yrthaks and screams. The bronze covering his body shatters to reveal a mortal warrior pierced through the heart by a githyanki arrow, who whispers, "Do not let the city fall," before toppling over dead.

Sign: Your skin takes on a metallic bronze sheen, your muscles tighten, and your posture becomes proud and upright.

Influence: You must always intercede on behalf of innocents when their lives are threatened, slay githyanki on sight whenever the opportunity presents itself, and never retreat from a fight if leaving would endanger innocents.

Granted Abilities:
When you bind Zuriel, you can transform into a bronze-skinned juggernaut, unleash quickened force spells, double your Pact Augmentation bonuses, and shield allies with your own life.

Bronze Body: As a swift action, you can activate a Tenser's Transformation effect (+4 enhancement bonus to Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution, +4 natural armor bonus to AC, +5 competence bonus on Fortitude saves, proficiency with all simple and martial weapons, base attack bonus equal to your character level/Hit Dice including extra iterative attacks and natural weapon claw/bite routines, and a strict lockout of spells, psionics, invocations, utterances, maneuvers, spell-like abilities, and non-potion item activations while allowing supernatural abilities) for up to 1 round per effective binder level per day (split across multiple uses as desired). Ending or exhausting this effect starts a 5-round cooldown before you can activate it again. You cannot use Forceful Speed of Thought while Bronze Body is active.

Forceful Speed of Thought: Once every 5 rounds (while Bronze Body is not active), you can use one of three quickened supernatural force abilities: Quickened Empowered Maximized Magic Missile (5 base missiles plus up to 2 Force Missile Mage bonus missiles dealing 5 + 1/2 of 1d4+1 [6-7] force damage each, ignoring spell resistance and rolling Force Missile Mage penetration checks against Shield), Quickened Wall of Force (creates an immovable wall of force barrier lasting 1 round per effective binder level; destroyed by Disintegrate or a Sphere of Annihilation, or activate again to dismiss), or Quickened Forcecage (immobilizes and blocks teleportation for all creatures within a 10-foot radius for 2 hours per effective binder level; destroyed by Disintegrate or a Sphere of Annihilation, or activate again to dismiss). Only one Forceful Speed of Thought effect can be in place at one time; while a Wall of Force or Forcecage is active, you cannot use the other two options until its duration expires or you dismiss it.

Pact Fortification: Zuriel intensifies the bonds you forge when making pacts. All bonuses you receive from your Pact Augmentation class feature are doubled (for example, +10 HP per selection instead of +5 HP, or DR 2/- per selection instead of DR 1/-).

Ultimate Sacrifice: As an immediate action, you can ward a visible ally (other than yourself) within long range so that 100% of the damage dealt to that ally by the next single attack, spell, or ability is redirected to you instead (preserving the exact per-damage-type breakdown across all 13 damage types), leaving your ally unharmed even if the transferred damage kills you.
