:: Life Fades, Greater ::

Master, Breath of Twilight
Level/School: 7th/Necromancy
Range: Close (25 ft. + 5 ft./2 levels)
Target: One living creature/level in a 20-ft.-radius spread
Duration: Instantaneous
Saving Throw: Fortitude partial
Spell Resistance: Yes

Draining shadows erupt around your foes, funneling their essence and energy into the Plane of Shadow.

Your touch deals 1d6 points of damage per caster level (maximum 20d6) and causes the subject to become exhausted for 1 round per caster level (a Fortitude save reduces the damage by half and decreases the exhaustion to fatigue).
