:: Fast Healing ::

Type of Feat: Class
Prerequisite: Forsaker 1
Specifics: Forsakers regain hit points at an exceptionally fast rate. At 1st level, the character regains 1 hit point per round, to a maximum of 10 hit points per day. The number of hit points regained per round increases by +1 for every four forsaker levels, and the maximum restorable per day increases by 10 for every two forsaker levels. When triggered, heals the maximum amount for the day.
Use: Selected
