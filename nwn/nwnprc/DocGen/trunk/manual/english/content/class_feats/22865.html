:: Lightning Punch ::

Type of Feat: Legacy
Prerequisite: Greater Legacy Ritual
Benefit: Beginning at 19th level, once per day on command, you can charge Bullybasher's Gauntlets with chain lightning. The next creature struck by the gauntlets is the target of the spell, and secondary bolts can strike other foes within 30 feet. A charge is maintained until the gauntlets strike a creature. The save DC is 19, or 16 + your Charisma modifier, whichever is higher. Caster level 15th.
Use: Selected
