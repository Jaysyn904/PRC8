:: Smite Chaos ::

Type of Feat: Class
Prerequisite: Sapphire Hierarch 1
Specifics: Beginning at 1st level, you can smite chaos once per day. This ability is identical to the paladin's smite evil class feature, except that the bonuses apply against chaotic creatures rather than evil ones. At 4th level, and at every three levels thereafter, you can smite chaos one additional time per day.
Use: Selected
