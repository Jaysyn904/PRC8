:: Eldritch Disruption ::

Type of Feat: Shadowcasting
Prerequisite: Noctumancer 6
Specifics: At 6th level, you can use a standard action to disrupt the magical energies of a single target within 60 feet. That target must make a Will saving throw (DC 10 + your noctumancer level + your Cha modifier) or take a -4 penalty to its caster level for any mysteries or spells it casts for 1 minute.

You can use eldritch disruption three times per day.
Use: Selected.
