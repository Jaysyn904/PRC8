:: Fiendslaying ::

Type of Feat: Class Specific
Prerequisite: Knight of the Chalice Level 1
Specifics: Knights of the Chalice gain a number of special benefits in combat with evil outsiders. A 1st-level knight of the Chalice gets a +1 competence bonus on attack rolls against evil outsiders. On a successful attack, she deals an extra 1d6 points of damage due to her expertise in combating these creatures.

These bonuses increase as the knight advances in level, as shown below.

Level Enhancement / Damage
1 +1/+1d6 Physical
3 +2/+2d6 Physical
6 +3/+2d6 Physical and +1d6 Divine
9 +4/+2d6 Physical and +2d6 Divine
11 +5/+3d6 Physical and +2d6 Divine

Pattern repeats through epic levels.

Use: Automatic.

Feat Replaced By:

Fiendslaying
