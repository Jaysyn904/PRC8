:: Shocking Shot ::

Type of Feat: Legacy
Prerequisite: Lesser Legacy Ritual
Benefit: Beginning at 14th level, as a standard action, you can imbue an arrow nocked on the Bow of the Black Archer with electricity. If your shot with this arrow hits, the arrow deals an extra 5d6 points of electricity damage. This ability can be used five times per day, and a use is wasted if the charged arrow misses.
Use: Selected
