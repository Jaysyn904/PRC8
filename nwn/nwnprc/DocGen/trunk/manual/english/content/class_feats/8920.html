:: Incandescent Strike Invest ::

Type of Feat: Class
Prerequisite: Incandescent Champion 1
Specifics: You can channel incarnum to increase the damage dealt by your melee attacks. For each such augmented attack, you gain a bonus on your damage roll equal to the number of points of essentia invested in this ability. Whenever you have essentia invested in incandescent strike, your hands glow like a light spell.
Use: Selected
