:: Pierce the Black Heart ::

Type of Feat: Legacy
Prerequisite: Greater Legacy Ritual
Benefit: You and the Bow of the Black Archer become the ultimate expression of Shevarash's fury. At 20th level and higher, once per day, you can fire an arrow from the Bow of the Black Archer that kills any drow struck by it, as if by a finger of death spell. You must declare that you are using this ability before making the attack. If the arrow misses, the effect is wasted for the day. The save DC is 20, or 17 + your Charisma modifier, whichever is higher.
Use: Selected
