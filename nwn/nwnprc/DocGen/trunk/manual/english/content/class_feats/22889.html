:: Silver Starlight ::

Type of Feat: Legacy
Prerequisite: Greater Legacy Ritual
Benefit: Beginning at 17th level, once per day on command, you can summon forth up to four beams of painfully bright, cold light. This ability functions much like sunbeam, but the light is pure, focused starlight and moonlight. The beam deals no extra damage to undead. Instead, lycanthropes take damage from the beam as if they were undead being affected by a normal sunbeam spell. Fungi, mold, oozes, and slimes still take extra damage (as if they were lycanthropes). The save DC is 20, or 17 + your Charisma modifi er, whichever is higher. Caster level 15th.
Use: Selected
