:: Body Fuel ::

Type of Feat: Psionic

You can expand your power point total at the expense of your health.

Specifics: You can recover 2 power points by taking 1 point of ability burn damage to each of your three ability scores: Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution. You can recover additional power points for a proportional cost; for example, you could choose to recover 6 power points by taking 3 points of ability burn damage to Strength, Dexterity, and Constitution. These recovered points are added to your power point reserve as if you had gained them by resting overnight.

Special: Only living creatures can use this feat. You can take advantage of this feat only while in your own body (if you are under the effect of mind switch or metamorph, for example, you gain no benefit).

Use: Selected.

Requires All of These Feats:

Psionic Focus
