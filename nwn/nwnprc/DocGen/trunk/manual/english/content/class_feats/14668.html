:: Archivist Spiderskin ::

Caster Level(s): Druid 3, Sorc/Wiz 3
Innate Level: 3
School: Transmutation
Descriptor(s):
Component(s): Verbal, Somatic
Range: Touch
Area of Effect / Target: Creature touched
Duration: 10 min / level
Additional Counter Spells:
Save: None
Spell Resistance: No

The spell grants the recipient a +1 enhancement bonus to its existing natural armor bonus, a +1 racial bonus on saves against poison, and a +1 racial bonus on Hide checks for every three caster levels you have. This means that the bonuses to natural armor, saves, and Hide checks improve to +2 at caster level 6th, +3 at caster level 9th, +4 at 12th, and +5 at 15th.

The enhancement bonus provided by spiderskin stacks with the subject's natural armor bonus*, but not with other enhancement bonuses to natural armor. A creature without natural armor has an effective natural armor bonus of +0, much as a character wearing only normal clothing has an armor bonus of +0.

Arcane Material Component: The leg of a spider.

*Not currently implemented
