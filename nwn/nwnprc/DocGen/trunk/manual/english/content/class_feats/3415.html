COMBAT MANEUVER
End Grapple - Ends any grapple you initiated

Fight Defensively - You gain a +2 Dodge bonus to AC, and take a -4 penalty on all attack rolls. If you have 5 or more ranks in Tumble, increase your Dodge AC by +1.
