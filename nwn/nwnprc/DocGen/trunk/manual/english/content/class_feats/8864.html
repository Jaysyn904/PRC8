:: Smite Opposition ::

Type of Feat: Class
Prerequisite: Soulborn 1
Specifics: Once per day you can channel the power of your incarnum-fueled convictions to attempt to smite a foe with a melee attack. You add your Charisma bonus (if any) to your attack roll and deal 1 extra point of damage per soulborn level. These bonuses apply only against creatures whose alignment opposes at least one component of yours-a lawful good soulborn smites chaotic and evil foes (any creature whose alignment includes either chaos or evil), while a chaotic good soulborn smites lawful and evil foes. If you accidentally smite a creature of the wrong alignment, the smite has no effect, but the ability is still used up for that day. At 5th level and every five levels thereafter, you can smite opposition one additional time per day to a maximum of five times per day at 20th level.
Use: Selected
