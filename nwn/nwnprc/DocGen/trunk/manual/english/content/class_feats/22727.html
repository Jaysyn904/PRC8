:: Winged Range ::

Type of Feat: Legacy
Prerequisite: Least Legacy Ritual
Benefit: At 6th level and higher, you can cast up to three arcane spells per day of up to 3rd level that are extended as though using the Extend Spell metamagic feat. At 12th level, you can extend three arcane spells per day that are 6th level or lower.
Use: Selected
