:: Reflections of Things to Come ::

Master, Eyes of the Night Sky
Level/School: 9th/Divination
Range: Personal
Target: You
Duration: 10 minutes/level

Using the greatest of magic, you peer through the Plane of Shadow back into the Material Plane, and view shadows and reflections of events that have not yet happened.

This mystery grants you knowledge of what will occur (or at least what is likely to occur), granting you several benefits. You gain the uncanny dodge ability, a +10 bonus to Armor Class, and the improved initiative feat.
