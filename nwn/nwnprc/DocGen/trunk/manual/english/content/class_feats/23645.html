:: Ephemeral Image ::

Master, Dark Metamorphosis
Level/School: 7th/Illusion (Shadow)
Effect: One shadow duplicate
Duration: 1 minute/level
Saving Throw: None
Spell Resistance: No

You detach your own shadow and animate it with extraplanar energies, creating a dark-hued, hazy duplicate of yourself.

This mystery functions like the spell project image, except as noted above. If the image exceeds 100 feet + 10 ft per caster level from the caster, it ceases to function.
