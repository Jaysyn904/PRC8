You have transcended the common stage to become a living legend of performance. Every setting—be it a grand theater or a chance encounter on the road—transforms into your arena, where your magnetic presence and unparalleled charm captivate audiences beyond mortal expectation.

Hit Die: d6
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Intelligence modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Virtuoso gains a bonus feat every two levels

Special:
Spells: The epic virtuoso increases caster level by one per level gained above 10th.
Bard Song: The epic virtuoso's bard song continues into epic levels.

Epic Virtuoso Bonus Feat List:
Blinding Speed, Combat Casting, Curse Song, Epic Leadership, Epic Reputation, Epic Skill Focus, Epic Spell Focus, Epic Spellcasting, Extra Music, Great Charisma, Great Intelligence, Improved Combat Casting, Lasting Inspiration, Legendary Commander, Lingering Song, Master Wand, Tenacious Magic.
