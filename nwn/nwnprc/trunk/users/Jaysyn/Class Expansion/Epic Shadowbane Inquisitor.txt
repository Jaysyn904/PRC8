A paragon of righteous fury, the epic Shadowbane Inquisitor is a relentless force against darkness. Wielding divine might, they expose and annihilate evil with overwhelming power. 

Hit Die: d10
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Shadowbane Inquisitor gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Sneak Attack: The epic Shadowbane Inquisitor continues gaining sneak attack increases at the raite of 1d6 every three class levels.
Smite: The epic Shadowbane Inquisitor continues to gain another use of Smite every four class levels.
