Epic Witchborn Binders are the final bulwark against arcane tyranny. Having mastered the fusion of incarnum and anti-magic, they become living wards, nullifying spellcraft with a gesture, and shackling even the most powerful wizards in prisons of soulstuff. At this level, their presence alone can suppress magical auras, unravel arcane constructs, and seal away reality-bending threats. Vigilant and relentless, an epic Witchborn Binder is both a hunter and a sentinel, wielding pure incarnum as a weapon of justice.

Hit Die: d6.
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Intelligence modifier.
Bonus Feats: The epic Witchborn Binder gains a bonus feat every three levels.

Special:
Meldshaping: Meldshaper level increases as if gaining a level in your meldshaper class at each Witchborn Binder class level.

Epic Witchborn Binder Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Epic Energy Resistance, Epic Fortitude, Epic Prowess, Epic Reflexes, Epic Reputation, Epic Skill Focus, Epic Toughness, Epic Will, Great Constitution, Superior Initiative, Bonus Soulmeld, Epic Essentia, Extra Chakra Bind, Open Heart Chakra, Open Soul Chakra, Rapid Meldshaping, Rebind Soulmeld.
