At the epic scale, the Diabolist no longer imitates the damned—they command them. Every whispered invocation sears the Weave with the echoes of Hell's will, and each spell drips with the essence of infernal corruption. They walk among mortals cloaked in fire and dread, their ambition smoldering like embers beneath skin. Devils no longer tempt the Diabolist—they envy them. For what greater blasphemy than a mortal who dares not serve, but instead seeks to ascend?

Hit Die: d4
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Diabolist gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Diabolism: The epic Diabolist's Diabolism continues to increase by +1d6 every 3 class levels.
Spellcasting: The epic Diabolist continues to increase in arcane caster level (and spells known, if applicable).
