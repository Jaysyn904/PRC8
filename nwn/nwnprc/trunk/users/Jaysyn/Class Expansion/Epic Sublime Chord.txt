At the pinnacle of their mastery, a Sublime Chord transcends mere music and magic, unraveling the fundamental song of creation. As they progress, their understanding of the universal harmony grants them access to increasingly powerful and otherworldly effects. From harnessing arcane power through song to manipulating time itself, a Sublime Chord becomes a conduit of cosmic forces. 

Hit Die: d6
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Sublime Chord gains a bonus feat every four levels.

Special:
Bardic Abilities: The epic Sublime Chord continues to increase their Bardic Lore & Bardic Song abilities.
Spellcasting: The epic Sublime Chord continues to increase in caster level (and spells known, if applicable).

Epic Sublime Chord Bonus Feat List:
Automatic Quicken Spell, Automatic Silent Spell, Automatic Still Spell, Blinding Speed, Epic Fortitude, Epic Leadership, Epic Skill Focus, Epic Spell Focus, Epic Spell Penetration, Epic Spellcasting, Epic Reflexes, Epic Will, Great Charisma, Great Dexterity, Ignore Material Components, Improved Combat Casting, Lasting Inspiration, Legendary Commander.
