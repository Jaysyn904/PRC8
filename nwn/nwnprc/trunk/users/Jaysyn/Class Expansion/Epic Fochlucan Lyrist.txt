An Epic Fochlucan Lyrist is the embodiment of harmony between sword, spell, and song. As a master of both arcane and divine magic, their knowledge of lore and music transcends mortal understanding. They weave powerful enchantments into their melodies, bolster allies with ancient wisdom, and strike down foes with finesse and supernatural prowess. 

Hit Die: d6
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 6 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Fochlucan Lyrist gains a bonus feat every five levels

Special:
Bardic Abilities: The epic Fochlucan Lyrist continues to increase their Bardic Lore and Bardic Song abilities.
Spellcasting: The epic Fochlucan Lyrist continues to increase in arcane as well as divine caster level (and spells known, if applicable).

Epic Fochlucan Lyrist Bonus Feat List:
Automatic Quicken Spell, Automatic Silent Spell, Automatic Still Spell, Blinding Speed, Curse Song, Dragon Wild Shape, Epic Energy Resistance, Epic Leadership, Epic Prowess, Epic Reputation, Epic Skill Focus, Epic Spell Focus, Epic Spell Penetration, Epic Spellcasting, Extra Music, Fast Healing, Focused Spell Penetration, Great Charisma, Great Wisdom, Ignore Material Components, Improved Combat Casting, Lasting Inspiration, Legendary Commander, Lingering Song, Master Wand, Perfect Health, Plant Wild Shape, Tenacious Magic.
