An Epic Disciple of Mephistopheles becomes a living conduit of hellfire, their body and soul twisted by infernal power. Their mere presence scorches the earth, their voice echoes with embers, and flames answer their every command. No longer just a servant, they are a herald of Mephistopheles himself—incinerating armies with infernal storms, summoning greater devils as lieutenants, and walking untouched through magical and mundane flame alike. Their mastery of hellfire transcends mortal understanding, making them a terror to celestials and mortals alike.

Hit Die: d8
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Disciple of Mephistopheles gains a bonus feat every other level.

Epic Disciple of Mephistopheles Bonus Feat List:
Armor Skin, Blinding Speed, Epic Damage Reduction, Epic Energy Resistance,  Epic Prowess, Epic Reflexes, Epic Reputation, Epic Toughness, Epic Weapon Focus, Epic Will, Fast Healing, Great Charisma, Great Constitution, Improved Whirlwind Attack, Legendary Commander, Superior Initiative.
