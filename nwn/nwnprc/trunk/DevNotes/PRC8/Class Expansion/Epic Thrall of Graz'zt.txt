Epic Thralls of Graz’zt become living conduits of seduction and destruction. Their dark charisma becomes irresistible, allowing them to manipulate even the most stalwart minds. Their spell betrayal abilities reach deadly precision, turning every opening into an opportunity for pain. With the power to summon greater demons and twist arcane energy into lethal cunning, an Epic Thrall of Graz'zt no longer merely follows the Dark Prince—they act as his voice, his blade, and his will.

Hit Die: d6
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 4 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Thrall of Graz'zt gains a bonus feat every four levels

Special:
Dark Charisma: the epic Thrall of Graz'zt's Dark Charisma ability contiues to increase its bonus by +2 every odd level.
Spell Betrayal: the epic Thrall of Graz'zt's Spell Betrayal ability continues to increase its bonus by +1d6 every even level.
Spellstrike: the epic Thrall of Graz'zt's Spellstrike ability continues to increase its bonus by +1d6 every even level.
Spellcasting: The epic Thrall of Graz'zt continues to increase in arcane caster level (and spells known, if applicable).
