At epic levels, your mastery of both arcane and divine magic crowns you as an exalted Hathran —a beacon of healing and leadership in Rashemen. You embody the sacred legacy of the learned sisters, extending your nurturing miracles and commanding spells that both protect your people and instill awe in your foes.

Hit Die: d4
Skill Points at Each Additional Level: 2 + Int Modifier
Bonus Feats: The epic Hathran gains a bonus feat every three levels

Special:
Spells: The epic Hathran increases divine and arcane caster level by one every other level gained above 10th.
